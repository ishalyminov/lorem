# Privacy Policy for Lorem

**Last updated: June 4, 2026**

Lorem ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

Lorem does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. Specifically:

- **No accounts or sign-ups** — the App does not require you to create an account.
- **No analytics or tracking** — the App does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry.
- **No cloud storage** — all your data is stored locally on your device.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally in its database on your device:

- Reminder titles and locations (latitude, longitude, location name)
- Proximity radius settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the **Export** feature in Settings to save a JSON file to a location of your choosing.

## Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS and location services to check whether you are near a saved reminder location. Location data is processed entirely on-device and is **never** sent to any server.

The App requests the following permissions:

- **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION** — to determine your current position relative to your reminders.
- **ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION** — to monitor your location and trigger notifications while the App is in the background.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE** — to run a background service that checks your proximity to reminder locations.

You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings. The App will not function for location monitoring if permissions are denied.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the **Google Maps SDK** to display map imagery and provide place search. Google may collect usage data per [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App does not share any user data with Google beyond what is required by the Maps SDK itself.

## Data Deletion

All data can be deleted by uninstalling the App, which removes the local database entirely. You may also delete individual reminders within the App at any time.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
